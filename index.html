Landing Cost Deviation Guard
Jul 2025
Shipped

Operators sometimes typed product IDs into the landing price field, which inflated cost of goods sold. I built a check that compares each new cost against 7 days of historical rates. Changes of 0–10% are accepted, 10–50% show a warning, and anything over 50% is blocked unless a Super-Admin overrides it. It works on shipments and on CSV imports, with colour-coded preview rows.
